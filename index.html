About

I have 3+ years of experience using Python and query languages to extract, process, and analyze data to support decision-making. Over time, my work evolved from querying APIs and databases to support users, to building more complex analyses and dashboards for stakeholders, and eventually to automating those processes and building data pipelines and analytics systems.

I’m naturally curious and enjoy solving difficult problems, understanding how things work, and using data to make sense of the world. I like building systems that are efficient, well-designed, and useful, while understanding the business or processes behind the data.

My current focus is data engineering and analytics, while I’m building a broader, end-to-end understanding of the data stack, from how data is generated and processed to how it can be analyzed and used in ML and AI applications. I care about understanding the fundamentals, not just the tools.

Outside of work, I enjoy sports, traveling, reading about philosophy or history, and spending time with family and friends.

Here are some of the specific areas and skills I’ve worked with throughout my career:

Product analytics

Data pipelines

Dashboards & reporting

Data engineering

AI & LLMs

Dash apps

NBA Analytics

An interactive basketball analytics app built in Python with Dash and Plotly, born out of a passion for the Philadelphia 76ers. It lets anyone explore team and player performance across seasons and metrics instead of reading static box scores. Data comes from the community-maintained nba_api, lands in BigQuery, and refreshes daily with Cloud Functions and Cloud Scheduler; the dashboard queries BigQuery directly with SQL. Deployed on Render, so the free tier may take a minute to wake up on first load.
